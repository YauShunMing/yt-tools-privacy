Shunming Video Channel Manager

This is a personal, internal tool used by its owner (Simon Yau) to manage a small set of YouTube channels that he owns and operates — uploading videos, setting thumbnails, and adding caption tracks via the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public product or service, has no external end users, and is not offered commercially. It exists solely to automate the owner's own channel-publishing workflow.
